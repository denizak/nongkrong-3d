Privacy notice

Last updated: 14 November 2026

This page explains the data flows implemented by Nongkrong 3D. The operator’s contact for privacy questions is webmaster@denizakya.online. The operator’s identity, applicable legal bases, service providers, retention and international transfers still need confirmation to complete this notice.

Playing and browser storage

The game stores your display name, robot appearance, game progress, inventory, fish collection, visited islands, language, audio and quality preferences, voice settings and, when used, a private-room secret in your browser's local storage. These are stored on your device until cleared or replaced, subject to your browser's storage policies. Your browser's site-data controls can remove them; this also deletes local game progress and preferences.

Multiplayer and chat

When you start multiplayer, the game uses Trystero and Nostr signalling relays to discover and connect to other players. Your display name, robot appearance, character position, pose and chat messages are sent to connected players. Peers and connection services may see your IP address and connection metadata. A configured TURN service may relay traffic when direct connections cannot be made. Relay, TURN, hosting and network providers may process connection data under their own operational practices.

You can choose Play solo on the start screen to avoid the game's multiplayer discovery, chat transmission and voice connections. Loading the site and its assets still requires a connection to its hosting service.

Voice

Voice chat is optional. The browser asks for microphone permission when you first use it. When you talk, audio is sent to selected nearby players using WebRTC. Other players can hear it and may record it independently. You can release the talk control or switch open mic off; an idle microphone is released after about 30 seconds while the game is running.

Private rooms

A private-room invite contains a secret in its URL fragment. Anyone with that link can join, so only share it with people you trust. The room secret is remembered in your browser. Room signalling is encrypted; network services can still receive connection metadata.

Open information and your rights

Where applicable, data protection law gives you rights concerning your personal data, including access, correction, deletion, objection and complaint to a supervisory authority. For privacy questions or requests, contact webmaster@denizakya.online. The operator must confirm which external providers are used, their locations and retention periods, and the legal bases for processing.
